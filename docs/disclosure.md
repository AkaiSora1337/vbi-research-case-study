# Public Disclosure Boundary

## Included

- Original explanatory text written for this case study.
- High-level diagrams of the research and verification workflow.
- General descriptions of numerical methods and evidence standards.
- Selected aggregate numerical results and newly generated figures, with their scope and definitions.
- A research poster and its editable PowerPoint version.
- A transparent statement of individual and collaborative boundaries.

## Intentionally excluded

- The collaborative project's MATLAB source code.
- Original or derived bridge-model files.
- Vehicle libraries, traffic inputs, and raw simulation results.
- Engineering drawings, books, papers, and extracted figures.
- Internal file paths, machine logs, credentials, hashes, and environment records.
- Unreviewed raw output and unpublished material beyond the selected aggregate case-study results.
- Bridge drawings and source reference materials provided by Professor Zhang.
- Any private framework material authored by another contributor unless separately licensed or explicitly approved.

## Rationale

The purpose of this repository is to demonstrate research judgment and technical communication, not to reproduce a private research codebase. Readers can evaluate how the work was framed, checked, and reported without receiving materials that the repository owner may not have the right to redistribute.

The reconstruction, implementation, numerical verification, simulation review, and documentation described in the case study were completed by the repository owner. Excluding source materials is an ownership and confidentiality decision, not an indication that those tasks were completed by someone else.
