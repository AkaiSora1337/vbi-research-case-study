# Poster Abstract

## Concrete Bridge Deck and Slab Fatigue under Lane-Free Traffic

Assessment Using a Vehicle–Bridge Interaction Framework

**Wang Yanjun**  
Civil, Environmental and Resources Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, Kyoto University

The November 2026 vehicle–bridge interaction campaign completed 22 traffic scenarios. Maximum conditional compression damage was 1.41587 × 10⁻¹³ for the two-lane reference and 6.81051 × 10⁻¹⁴ for flocking. Flocking had 11 tensile exceedance locations and 17 point events. The two-lane reference had zero. Interior sampled-to-plate-centre peak ratios across the campaign were 99.87–104.38% in tension and 99.56–102.03% in compression. The compression index uses an extrapolated S–N curve. The November comparison remains preliminary pending further solver verification.

Professor Kai-Chun Chang provided the bridge drawings and source reference materials.
