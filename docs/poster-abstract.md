# Poster Abstract

## Concrete Bridge Deck and Slab Fatigue under Lane-Free Traffic

Assessment Using a Vehicle–Bridge Interaction Framework

**Wang Yanjun**  
Civil, Environmental and Resources Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, Kyoto University

This study compares 22 vehicle–bridge simulations with a common vehicle fleet. Elastic-model envelopes show where principal tension and deflection peak in the flocking reference. Top-face tension follows the girder lines, whereas bottom-face tension concentrates between them. In a separate check using recovered concrete stresses, flocking produced 11 tensile exceedance locations and 17 point events. The two-lane reference recorded zero. The poster compares the spatial response and explains the tensile and compression checks. Results remain preliminary pending further solver verification.

Professor Kai-Chun Chang provided the bridge drawings and source reference materials.
