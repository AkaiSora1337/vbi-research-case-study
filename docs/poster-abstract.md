# Poster Abstract

## Concrete Bridge Deck and Slab Fatigue under Lane-Free Traffic

Assessment Using a Vehicle–Bridge Interaction Framework

**Wang Yanjun**  
Civil, Environmental and Resources Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, Kyoto University

The 29–30 October 2026 vehicle–bridge interaction campaign completed 22 traffic scenarios. Maximum conditional compression damage was 1.24296 × 10⁻¹³ for the two-lane reference and 6.1632 × 10⁻¹⁴ for flocking. Flocking had 10 tensile exceedance locations and 16 point events. The two-lane reference had zero. Interior sampled-to-plate-centre peak ratios across the campaign were 99.86–104.45% in tension and 99.71–102.05% in compression.

Professor Kai-Chun Chang provided the bridge drawings and source reference materials.
