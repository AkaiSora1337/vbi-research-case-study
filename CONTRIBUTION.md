This case study describes my individual contribution within a vehicle–bridge interaction research project.

## Individual contribution

I independently completed the following work:

- reconstructing and organizing an equivalent bridge-analysis workflow;
- designing numerical verification and convergence checks;
- reviewing long-duration simulation outputs for completeness and consistency;
- improving traceability between assumptions, model inputs, numerical results, and conclusions;
- documenting limitations and preventing diagnostic results from being presented as physical validation.
- extending nominal reinforcement-section recovery at saved observation coordinates and auditing the corresponding numerical implementation.
