The poster uses a single A3 landscape page (420 × 297 mm) for online reading. Its text and data chart remain editable in PowerPoint. The PDF can scale proportionally for larger printing.

The poster presents the traffic comparison, peak-range coverage and numerical section-recovery checks. Professor Kai-Chun Chang provided the bridge drawings and source references. The screening indices do not establish experimental validity or fatigue life.
