The poster uses a single A3 landscape page (420 × 297 mm) for online reading. Its text and data chart remain editable in PowerPoint. The PDF can scale proportionally for larger printing.

The central figure reports the 23 October gross-section sampling study. The section-recovery note summarizes the separate 26 October extension. Preliminary numerical findings do not establish experimental validity or fatigue life.
